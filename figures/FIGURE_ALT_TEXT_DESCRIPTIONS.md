### Main Manuscript Figures

#### Figure 1
*Alt-text:* Schematic methodological framework diagram illustrating data sources, variables, frontier modeling techniques, econometric estimators, and sequential workflow.

#### Figure 2
*Alt-text:* High-resolution GIS map of Türkiye displaying five color-coded maritime fishing basins and all 386 coastal fisheries facilities classified by type (236 commercial fishing ports as black circles, 72 slipways as orange triangles, and 78 shelters as cyan diamonds), with a caveat note regarding approximate coordinates and unrecorded artisanal slipways, major landing hubs, dominant gears, and regional landing shares.

#### Figure 3
*Alt-text:* Four-panel line graph illustrating 2000–2024 annual trajectories for landings volume, real catch value, vessel counts, and fleet tonnage across Türkiye's five maritime basins, highlighting the 2012–2018 vessel decommissioning period.

#### Figure 4
*Alt-text:* Three-panel display illustrating technology adoption heatmap, modernization index trajectories showing rapid electronic adoption across all basins, and rolling correlations oscillating around zero, indicating decoupling from capacity utilization.

#### Figure 5
*Alt-text:* Grouped bar chart comparing CRS, VRS, and Scale Efficiency across five maritime basins, showing high pure technical efficiency in the Mediterranean and West Black Sea contrasted against pervasive scale inefficiency.

#### Figure 6
*Alt-text:* Two-panel figure with horizontal bar chart of mean regional capacity utilization and scatter plot of CU versus MI with regional 1.5 SD confidence ellipses and four annotated quadrants illustrating the concentration of modernized vessels in low-utilization quadrants.
