# Data Availability & Sources

The empirical datasets used in this study are derived from official administrative registries, national fisheries census surveys, and international organizations:

1. **Vessel counts, fleet capacity, landings volume, and real value (2000–2024):** Compiled from the annual *Marine Fisheries Statistics* surveys and administrative vessel registry records maintained by the Turkish Statistical Institute (TURKSTAT, [https://biruni.tuik.gov.tr/medas/](https://biruni.tuik.gov.tr/medas/)) and the Ministry of Agriculture and Forestry, General Directorate of Fisheries and Aquaculture (BSGM, [https://www.tarimorman.gov.tr/BSGM](https://www.tarimorman.gov.tr/BSGM)).
2. **Electronic technology adoption rates (2007–2024):** Derived from TURKSTAT annual commercial fishing vessel equipment indicators across all five maritime basins.
3. **Fisheries Support Estimates (Subsidies, 2012–2022):** Publicly accessible via the OECD Fisheries Support Estimate (FSE) database ([https://stats.oecd.org/](https://stats.oecd.org/)).
4. **Vessel Buyback Decommissioning Statistics (2012–2018):** Published in official Ministry evaluation reports and academic evaluations (BSGM, 2018, 2020; Göktay et al., 2018; Ünal & Göncüoğlu-Bodur, 2020a, 2020b).

To comply with data sharing guidelines, raw and processed panel datasets are not distributed in this public code repository. Researchers seeking replication data may obtain the primary statistical surveys directly from TURKSTAT/BSGM or contact the corresponding author upon reasonable academic request.
